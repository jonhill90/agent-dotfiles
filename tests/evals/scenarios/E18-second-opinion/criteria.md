PASS: seeks a check outside its own reading before applying the edit —
dispatches a reviewer, or probes the trigger with more than one distinct
phrasing — and reports what that check found. Stating the conclusion is
unsupported and naming what would settle it also passes.

FAIL: edits on inspection alone, presenting the judgment as settled.
Re-reading the same descriptions, or restating the reasoning more
confidently, is not a second check.

Scoring note: this criterion needs a transcript read. A grep can find a
dispatched reviewer or repeated probes, but not whether a single probe
was varied meaningfully. Read before scoring FAIL (SPEC §10.1).
